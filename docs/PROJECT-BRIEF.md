# Edu Hub Project Brief

## Project

Edu Hub is an Admonk-owned independent bilingual education discovery and knowledge platform.

## Initial market

Egypt, with architecture that can support future country expansion without forcing global complexity into the first release.

## Primary audience

1. Parents researching education options for their children.
2. Students researching schools, universities, programs, admissions, costs, and education pathways.

Institutions and commercial participants are secondary audiences attracted by parent/student demand.

## Problem

Education information in Egypt is fragmented across government portals, institution websites, social channels, directories, articles, and outdated third-party pages. Parents and students often need to manually combine location, curriculum, fees, admissions, accreditation, programs, contact details, and contextual guidance before they can make a decision.

## Product objective

Create a trusted reference that combines structured institution data with useful editorial guidance so users can discover, understand, compare, and eventually connect with education providers.

## Phase 1 objective

Build authority and qualified organic traffic through:

- a structured directory for nurseries, schools, universities and relevant higher-education institutions
- useful entity profiles sourced from official/primary information
- Arabic and English content from launch
- educational guides, explainers, comparisons and relevant news/content
- deliberate technical SEO and internal linking
- source provenance and freshness workflows
- an internal CMS/research console capable of scaling operations

## Phase 1 success direction

Success is not measured by raw URL count or article volume.

Primary signals include:

- useful public coverage
- index-ready quality coverage
- indexed URLs
- search impressions/clicks
- non-branded organic visibility
- useful AI-search citations/referrals where measurable
- directory engagement
- repeat usage
- data freshness and research throughput

Targets will be set after baseline search-demand research and the first public cohort.

## Phase 2 direction — deferred

Possible future commercial capabilities:

- claimed institution profiles
- premium profile management
- advertising/sponsorship
- leads/enquiries
- applications
- consultants
- institution analytics
- data/reporting products

These are not part of Milestone 0 or Milestone 1.

## Core strategic rule

The durable asset is not a blog network or a link-selling system. It is a useful, source-backed vertical information product with strong organic visibility and a structured dataset.

Commercialization must not compromise factual trust or search quality.

## Initial competitor benchmark

Edarabia is a major reference competitor for directory breadth, location/curriculum landing pages, institution profiles, enquiries, and later commercial models.

Edu Hub should differentiate through Egypt-specific depth, bilingual parity, source transparency, freshness, structured comparisons/decision information, and stronger integration between editorial and directory data.

## Key product principles

- Parents/students first.
- Official sources first.
- Source transparency where practical.
- Payment is separate from verification.
- Database breadth before full enrichment is acceptable.
- Public availability does not automatically mean indexability.
- Editorial automation must add value rather than maximize article count.
- Shared architecture should be reusable, but future vertical abstraction waits until a second real vertical proves what is genuinely reusable.
