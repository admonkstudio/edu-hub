# Edu Hub Project Brief

## Project

Edu Hub is an Admonk-owned independent bilingual education discovery and knowledge platform focused initially on **international education in Egypt**.

## Initial market

Egypt, with architecture that can support future country expansion without forcing global complexity into the first release.

## Primary audience

1. Parents researching international schools and education pathways for their children.
2. Students researching internationally scoped universities, programmes, admissions and costs.

Institutions and commercial participants are secondary audiences attracted by parent/student demand.

## Problem

Information about international education in Egypt is fragmented across school/university websites, accreditation and authorization bodies, regulators, admissions PDFs, social channels, maps and directories. Parents and students often need to manually reconcile whether an institution is genuinely international, which curriculum/accreditation applies, where its campuses are, what it costs, how admissions work and whether information is current.

Broad national coverage of every Egyptian public institution is not required to solve this problem and created disproportionate dependence on difficult government datasets. The product therefore prioritizes a smaller, higher-value and more verifiable international-education market.

## Product objective

Create the trusted reference for discovering, understanding and comparing international education options in Egypt by combining:

- canonical institution/campus identity;
- evidence-backed international eligibility;
- curriculum and accreditation data;
- location and contact information;
- admissions and academic-year fees;
- facilities and education levels;
- higher-education academic/programme structures where applicable;
- bilingual editorial guidance;
- publication-safe media and transparent source provenance.

## Active Phase 1 coverage

Include:

- private/independent international schools;
- private/independent IB World Schools;
- recognized British, American, French, German, Canadian and comparable international-school models;
- recognized foreign university branches;
- internationally chartered/accredited independent higher-education institutions where international status is substantive;
- eligible institution campuses and early-years sections.

Exclude:

- Egyptian public schools and universities;
- the national nursery universe;
- language schools/exam centres that do not have sufficient evidence of an international-school model;
- institutions classified as international only because of their name;
- Egyptian universities whose only international dimension is a partnership, exchange, dual degree or validated programme.

## Phase 1 objective

Build authority and qualified organic traffic through:

- a clean international-education registry rather than a mass scraped directory;
- useful bilingual institution and campus profiles;
- strong curriculum/accreditation comparisons;
- current fee/admission information where source-backed;
- useful guides, explainers and comparisons;
- technical SEO and internal linking;
- explicit source provenance and freshness workflows;
- an internal research/admin system capable of scaling updates.

## Phase 1 success direction

Success is not measured by raw URL count or maximum institution count.

Primary signals include:

- percentage of known international institutions represented;
- percentage with strong eligibility evidence;
- identity/campus deduplication quality;
- fee/admission/contact freshness;
- publication-safe media coverage;
- index-ready profile quality;
- search impressions/clicks and non-branded organic visibility;
- directory/filter/comparison engagement;
- repeat usage;
- research throughput and source-refresh reliability.

## Phase 2 direction — deferred

Possible future commercial capabilities:

- claimed institution profiles and institution-supplied media;
- premium profile management;
- advertising/sponsorship;
- leads/enquiries;
- applications;
- consultants;
- institution analytics;
- data/reporting products.

These do not change the factual verification standard.

## Core strategic rule

The durable asset is a useful, source-backed international-education knowledge graph and public discovery product—not a large scraped URL inventory.

Commercialization must remain separate from factual verification.

## Competitive direction

Broad directories such as Edarabia demonstrate demand for curriculum/location discovery and institution profiles. Edu Hub should differentiate through Egypt-specific international-education depth, bilingual parity, source transparency, current fees/admissions, campus accuracy, curriculum/accreditation evidence, rights-aware media and stronger structured comparisons.

## Key product principles

- Parents/students first.
- Evidence before claims.
- Institution and campus are distinct concepts.
- International status must be proven, not inferred from branding.
- Official regulator/authorization/accreditation and official institution sources are preferred.
- Supporting sources may enrich but cannot silently establish high-trust facts.
- Payment is separate from verification.
- Missing facts remain unknown rather than invented.
- Public availability does not automatically mean indexability.
- Public image availability does not automatically mean reuse rights.
- Editorial automation must add value rather than maximize article count.
