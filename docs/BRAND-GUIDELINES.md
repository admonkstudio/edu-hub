# Edu Hub Brand Guidelines

## Status

**Not yet defined.**

The working name `Edu Hub` is temporary. The final brand name, domain, identity, voice and visual direction must be developed in later lifecycle phases.

## Current approved brand facts

- The product is Admonk-owned.
- The public brand should be independent rather than presented as an Admonk service.
- Primary audiences are parents and students.
- The product should feel trustworthy, useful, current and easy to navigate.
- Arabic and English must receive equal design/editorial consideration from launch.
- Authority should come from clarity, source transparency and usefulness—not from looking governmental or institutional by default.

## Do not assume

Until creative direction is approved, agents must not invent or lock:

- logo
- colors
- typography
- illustration style
- photography style
- visual motifs
- tone-of-voice rules beyond clarity/trust
- motion language
- UI aesthetic

Do not copy Admonk's visual identity into Edu Hub merely because Admonk owns the product.
